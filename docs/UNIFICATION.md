# FOLKOOP unification

## Current controlling decision — 4 October 2026

Read [Foundation Charter](FOUNDATION_CHARTER.md), decision **FK-FOUNDATION-2026-10-04**, before applying older descriptions below or elsewhere.

FOLKOOP is one system built by extending the existing code and preserving the full functional intentions of four origins:

1. Sverinav — person to public infrastructure and authoritative next steps.
2. FOLKUNO — people, opportunities, Host/referral and BOTH online and physical models.
3. Cooperative-network work, including attributed KООПСЕТЬ source research — joint activity, demand, resources, work, production, purchases, sales and decisions.
4. ГБГ Форум — Göteborg-local community within Center.

Center may be online, physical or hybrid. Mura must illustrate the whole integrated product, including the Göteborg forum context, without registration inside her account. Blockchain is a required scoped design/prototype/integration workstream, not yet a shipped universal dependency.

No origin or existing supported capability may disappear silently during simplification, refactoring, short pitches or pilot selection. Pilot scope controls a test slice, not total product scope. See the charter for source recovery, requirement traceability, authority boundaries and explicit supersessions.

Former names may appear in historical/provenance explanations and source mapping. They remain origins/local-community names, not competing product brands. This clarification supersedes any blanket prohibition below on using origin names in active documentation.

## Earlier identity policy retained for provenance

FOLKOOP is the single current project identity.

Earlier standalone components were integrated into FOLKOOP and no longer exist as separate products in the current tree. Their technical provenance remains available in Git history and archived commit objects; current code, documentation, UI, rights notices and asset names use FOLKOOP terminology.

This policy does not rewrite historical commits, third-party names, external standards or source attributions. It only defines the current product identity.

The current modules — cooperation/network, City, Center, onboarding guide and supporting infrastructure — are parts of FOLKOOP. Internal module names describe function, not separate brands or separate licences.

The earlier statement that components were integrated means identity/partial technical integration, not proof that every source function has been implemented. Completion now requires the charter's coverage and acceptance process.

## Current-tree naming rule

FOLKOOP is the only current project and product identity. Functional names such as City, Center, People, Together, Projects, guide, outcome integrity and provenance describe parts of FOLKOOP. They are not separate products and do not have separate licences.

Do not revive predecessor product brands in runtime identifiers, package metadata or UI by accident. Preserve source attribution and allow explicit origin mapping in the current foundation/requirements documentation. Historical originals remain unmodified in Git history and private archives.
