# Optional civic activities — approved direction, not implemented

Goal: make a useful civic action understandable through a short objective and a few concrete steps. A game-like guide is not a mechanism to maximise complaints, political engagement or compulsive daily visits.

## First safe scope

Use existing neutral tasks: understand a road-holder result; prepare a factual report without sending duplicates; read an open municipal plan and draft a specific comment; understand the status of a Riksdag document. Start with authored instructions and voluntary completion in the current session. Avoid a marketplace or monetary points in the first release.

A guide should contain: title, purpose, approximate effort, prerequisite, geographic scope, current official source, 3–5 steps, safe stopping condition and original-service handoff. Steps may be skipped. Completion of preparation means preparation only, never proof that an authority accepted a case or fixed an issue.

## Evidence and progression

Separate learning, preparation, self-reported action, externally confirmed receipt and externally confirmed outcome. No fabricated “sent”, “accepted” or “resolved” label. Do not ask users to publish identity numbers, addresses or private case documents to prove participation. Anti-duplicate checks and source freshness must come before any rewards.

No rewards for a political viewpoint, votes, petition signatures, partisan persuasion, mass reporting or volume of authority submissions. Make help equally available regardless of opinions. Do not sell influence over official-source ranking. Sponsorship, if added, must be labelled and distinct from independent guidance.

No lost streak, shame notification, mandatory daily task or public leaderboard of civic virtue. A skipped day is not a failure. Let a useful notification save an app opening.

## Neighbour tasks and real rewards — later, separately gated

The wish to let neighbours or organisations propose help is recorded, not implemented. Monetary rewards, redeemable points, vouchers and ordinary volunteering are different models and need explicit terms. Points do not automatically remove employment, tax, consumer, safety or safeguarding obligations. Do not promise earnings or partner rewards before agreements/funding exist.

Do not launch unsupervised stranger-to-minor matching, private-home visits or dangerous road/repair/cleanup work. Require an age-appropriate scope, supervision, reporting/blocking, moderation, privacy minimisation and clear responsibility before accepting such tasks. Relevant starting source: Arbetsmiljöverket, https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/arbetsgivarens-ansvar-for-arbetsmiljon/minderariga-arbetstagare/ and AFS 2023:2 chapter 8. Applicability depends on the actual activity and organisation, not merely its label.

## Funding hypothesis

Keep residents' use free. Possible later funding: voluntary support, clearly separated organisational tools and transparently funded local initiatives. These are hypotheses, not contracts or a payment feature. Never monetise sensitive civic histories or reward data through profiling.

## Acceptance before the first guide ships

A tester understands the objective and completion status without assistance; can stop without losing progress or being pressured; can reach a current official source; does not confuse a completed lesson with a civic outcome. Preserve accessibility, translated labels and source/error states. Current v0.14 ships the editorial roadmap, not a guide engine.
