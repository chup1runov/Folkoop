These terms apply only to the first controlled, invite-only FOLKOOP Göteborg
core-loop pilot.

## 1. What you are joining

By accepting these terms, you ask the operator to provide access to the FOLKOOP
pilot service.

The pilot tests whether adults can:
- express a genuine low-risk need or offer;
- discover relevant people;
- join a cooperation;
- coordinate through FOLKOOP;
- complete a useful real-world action.

The pilot is experimental, limited and not a finished public service.

Participation is free of charge.

## 2. Who may participate

The first pilot is:
- invite-only;
- for adults aged 18 or older;
- limited to a small controlled Göteborg cohort.

An invitation does not guarantee permanent access.

## 3. What you may use the pilot for

Suitable examples include:
- language practice;
- simple digital/computer help;
- CV or portfolio feedback;
- local knowledge;
- simple translation help;
- low-risk creative/project work;
- organizing a small activity;
- sharing a low-value, non-regulated resource;
- finding someone for a small non-hazardous task.

The need or offer must be genuine.

## 4. What is outside the pilot

Do not use the pilot for:
- emergencies;
- medical diagnosis or treatment;
- professional legal representation;
- childcare;
- loans or cash transfers;
- housing or tenancy commitments;
- high-value goods;
- dangerous or regulated goods;
- hazardous work;
- electrical, gas or structural work requiring professional competence;
- political campaigning or persuasion;
- unlawful activity.

If a proposed use is ambiguous or high-risk, the operator may keep it outside
this pilot.

## 5. Your responsibility when cooperating

FOLKOOP helps people find and coordinate with one another. It does not guarantee:
- another participant's identity;
- competence or qualifications;
- safety;
- payment;
- delivery;
- availability;
- quality of work;
- completion of a real-world action.

Use ordinary judgment before meeting, lending items or relying on another
participant.

For emergencies or professional services, use appropriate real-world services.
