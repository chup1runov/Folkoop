# P0 PRE-PILOT COMPREHENSION GATE

## P0-01 — First-contact comprehension and Mura learning-space clarity

Status: **implemented and deployed** through PRs #136/#137 and final first-contact cleanup #152.

Trigger: repeated independent external first-contact feedback showed that users could not quickly identify FOLKOOP's purpose, compared it to an ordinary social network/group chat, and could mistake synthetic learning-space people for real participants.

Final contract:
- first contact is one Mura welcome surface rather than a language screen followed by a second choice screen;
- the surface explains `need / can offer / want to do -> relevant people/resources -> concrete next step`;
- all 11 languages are selectable directly on that surface;
- primary path: visit Mura's guided learning space;
- secondary path: sign in / register;
- illustrative people/activity are described as learning examples without persistent DEMO chrome;
- attempts to mutate learning data explain that the visitor is currently at Mura's place and must sign in to do the action in their own place;
- an explicit new visit to Mura restarts the 8-step tour from 1/8 even when an older browser already has onboarding completion stored;
- no new network capability or stronger matching claim is introduced.

Regression coverage:
- Chromium + WebKit;
- 11-language first-contact geometry;
- 390×844, 320×568, 844×390 and 1366×900 viewports;
- RTL Arabic/Persian and LTR Kurmanji;
- keyboard focus containment, short-screen reachability and fresh-screen scroll position.

# 6. Definition of project discipline

A mini-project is complete only when:

1. its trigger/problem is explicit;
2. implementation or operational work is finished;
3. relevant automated/manual verification passes;
4. docs reflect current reality;
5. no stronger claim is made than the evidence supports;
6. the next dependency is unblocked or explicitly documented.

The purpose of this plan is to make FOLKOOP easier to execute, not to create a larger backlog.
