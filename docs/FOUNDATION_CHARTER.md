# FOLKOOP — Foundation Charter v1.0

Decision: FK-FOUNDATION-2026-10-04. Owner instruction: 4 October 2026.
Status: ACCEPTED PRODUCT SCOPE; implementation remains incremental and must be evidenced.
Baseline: chup1runov/Folkoop @ c1a3dc8ef8d907a43809b78b975805ab015ef93b.

## 1. Non-negotiable purpose

FOLKOOP must integrate the existing implementation and the full functional intentions of four origins: Sverinav, FOLKUNO, cooperative-network work informed by Vitaly Tokarenko's KООПСЕТЬ, and ГБГ Форум. The origins are not four competing products or four disconnected tabs. Their capabilities must operate as one system. Neither a short pitch nor a small pilot may silently redefine the full product as only Need/Offer matching.

Canonical short definition supplied by the owner:

> FOLKOOP — единая сеть повседневной кооперации, которая связывает людей, сообщества, ресурсы, организации и возможности города и помогает им совместно решать задачи, создавать проекты и организовывать общественную и экономическую деятельность.

Expanded working definition (editorial clarification, not a replacement): FOLKOOP connects everyday social, civic and economic life through one cooperation network: people, conversations, communities, skills, resources, organisations, official processes, projects, collective decisions, economic activity, and online/physical places. It supports discovery, coordination, action, belonging and traceable outcomes. Mura illustrates this connected life. SDCF supplies a cross-cutting systems/decision/control discipline. The retained Web3/Web4 direction supplies identity, interoperability, agent and physical-world capabilities; blockchain is the required shared-trust workstream within that broader direction.

## 2. Authority and preservation

This charter is the controlling product-scope decision where older documents conflict with it. Code and tests describe what exists, not the maximum permitted future product scope. Existing privacy, security, source-truth and rights safeguards continue to apply.

The earlier rule "Göteborg core-loop first; feature breadth later" remains an implementation-sequencing tool, not permission to delete any origin or required capability. Pilot evidence determines implementation order and design, not unilateral cancellation of the owner's whole-system vision. A deferred requirement remains an obligation with a dependency, owner and review point. A proposed exclusion, narrowing or material semantic replacement requires an explicit owner decision recorded with its rationale.

Historical versions, rejected options and contradictions must remain traceable. Preserving an old idea does not silently reactivate outdated prices, quotas, legal assumptions or incompatible policies. Mark unresolved conflicts for decision; never call them implemented or quietly omit them.

## 3. Existing code is the integration base

Extend the current repository incrementally. Do not start a replacement application, erase the current database, rewrite the framework or discard working behavior merely to incorporate the four origins.

Preserve existing profiles/private drafts, People, Communities/publications, messaging/work chats, Need/Offer/Resource/Shared Purchase/Project objects, quantities/supplier coordination, tasks/assignees/updates, activity history, City adapters, Mura, all eleven languages, mobile/PWA behavior, accessibility and account/privacy/security controls. Each preservation claim must ultimately link to current code and regression tests. This document does not claim a fresh full-code audit.

## 3A. SDCF — cross-cutting systems, decision and control layer

SDCF is part of the FOLKOOP foundation as a **method/semantic layer**, not as another consumer-facing product tab or an automatic decision-maker. It is the owner's domain-independent systems/decision/control framework and should remain expressed in mainstream systems-engineering, decision, evidence, risk and learning terminology.

Preserve and map the SDCF kernel concepts across FOLKOOP where they add semantic discipline: System, Agent, Property, State, Event/Process, Objective/Operational Objective, Constraint, Criterion, Claim/Causal Claim, Prediction, Assumption, Model, Observation/Observation Result, Uncertainty Assessment, Plan, Decision, System Scope, Action and Controlled Action.

Required integration principles:
- objectives are made evaluable through criteria/metrics where appropriate;
- observations retain what was observed, about what, when and by whom/what;
- models retain purpose, scope, validity domain, version, status and provenance;
- predictions retain horizon, assumptions, support/evidence status and uncertainty;
- decisions retain considered alternatives, selected alternative, objective served, authority and time;
- controlled actions retain target and provenance to a decision, plan or controller;
- claims and evidence do not collapse into one truth flag;
- outcome, evidence and learning can update the system model without rewriting history.

Use SDCF to strengthen City routing/explanations, Center/Host decisions, projects, cooperative-economic coordination, outcome evaluation and future agent reasoning. It must not silently convert recommendations into official authority decisions, operator suggestions into user consent, or model output into verified real-world truth.

Known later SDCF work such as method routing and explicit learning-loop machinery remains in the no-loss source inventory. It becomes normative only after its source/version and acceptance tests are recovered and reviewed. Current SDCF artefacts must not be presented as proof of universal scientific validity merely because local/synthetic validation passes.

## 3B. Web3/Web4 capability family — retained full-scope direction

The already-approved Web3/Web4 architecture is preserved as part of the long-term FOLKOOP scope, subject to concrete product, privacy, security and interoperability triggers. It is infrastructure around the cooperation graph, not a separate product and not a requirement that ordinary participants learn Web3 terminology.

Preserve for future implementation:
- stable globally portable object identity/URN representation while UUID/PostgreSQL remain operational identity/truth;
- a derived Action/Cooperation Graph over normalized relational data, with provenance, rather than a premature second operational graph database;
- verifiable credentials, selective disclosure and cryptographic attestations where portable trust is useful;
- DID/EUDI or other wallet adapters only where a real identity/eligibility use case requires them;
- federation between independently operated Nodes/partners and selected public-social federation such as ActivityPub where justified;
- FOLKOOP Action-Agent tooling over reviewed APIs/RPCs;
- MCP and A2A interoperability for controlled tool/agent interaction;
- Place and Resource identities connected to the cooperation graph;
- QR/NFC for explicit physical-resource/place interactions;
- digital-twin state for Places/Resources where there is a real operational need;
- later IoT/access-system integration with proportional authorization;
- typed outcome/evidence attestations and external integrity anchoring.

Consequential agent actions require explicit authorization proportional to risk. No agent receives arbitrary SQL or service-role bypass. Federation introduces a remote trust/abuse boundary and must begin with explicit trust policy. Web3/Web4 implementation must preserve privacy/data minimisation and graceful operation when optional external trust/agent/physical systems are unavailable.

Blockchain remains governed by section 10 and ADR-002: it is the required design/prototype/integration workstream for a selected genuine shared-trust use case, not a replacement for this wider Web3/Web4 capability family.

## 4. Origin A — Sverinav: person ↔ public infrastructure

Preserve problem-first navigation, responsibility resolution, reporting preparation and official handoff, nearby/day-to-day information, public documents and processes, source-linked explanations, deadlines, followed changes and calendar routes. ## 6. Origin C — cooperative network: joint activity and economy

Preserve the full economic and organisational cycle, not just mutual help or group buying: aggregate demand, needs and offers; combine resources, skills, work and production; coordinate purchases AND sales; connect suppliers and buyers; coordinate storage, transport, distribution and returns; maintain projects, roles, tasks, agreements, community documents and collective decision-to-execution history; support coordinator education and shared-property/territorial initiatives.

Retain the source ideas concerning dues/contributions, budgets, accounting, service/dispatch requests, meter readings, land/resource maps and participatory processes. Their original institutional implementation is not automatically appropriate to FOLKOOP. Map each to a native capability, an explicit specialist integration or an unresolved adaptation requirement. "Not in first pilot" is not "removed".

Formal public authority, regulated financial custody, legally significant voting and specialist accounting remain with authorised systems where required. An integration is complete only when its user journey, permissions, source status, failure behavior and result are tested; a generic outbound link is not automatically full functional coverage.

KООПСЕТЬ is an attributed external source. This decision does not assert ownership of its code, a rights transfer or affiliation with its author.

## 7. Origin D — ГБГ Форум: local community within Center

ГБГ Форум is the Göteborg-local community component of Center. Center can therefore be ONLINE, PHYSICAL or HYBRID. A physical building is not a prerequisite for showing a real online community function.

Preserve the forum's ordinary conversation, existing topical structure, peer support, moderators, community norms, meetings, services and buy/sell/free exchange; add #ищу/#могу/#идея and human navigation without forcing all conversation into task objects. Preserve the distinction between forum conversation and an editorial channel/digest. Preserve participant input into substantial community changes.

A visitor who selects Göteborg should be able to discover this local Center context; do not infer precise residence or political interests. Other cities must not receive fabricated local forums. Integrating the product does not authorise renaming the external community, importing members/messages, linking identities, scraping conversations or sending announcements. Such operations require their own permissions and data-handling decisions.

The original forum and a corresponding FOLKOOP community surface can be connected deliberately without claiming that a live Telegram integration already exists.

Person ↔ Community ↔ Intent ↔ Need/Offer ↔ Skill/Resource ↔ Project/Task ↔ Organisation/Service ↔ City Process ↔ Center/Place/Activity ↔ Agreement/Decision ↔ Economic Coordination ↔ Outcome.

Local private drafts do not become public automatically. Formal legal membership is distinct from a user account or community participation.

## 9. Mura is the whole-system illustrative account

Mura must demonstrate the connected life of the entire integrated FOLKOOP, including all four origins as they become available. She is not just a tooltip mascot or an isolated feature tour.

Preserve the flow: honest illustrative-story disclosure → enter Mura → freely explore her people, conversations, communities, needs/offers/resources, drafts, projects/tasks, city processes, Center and outcome stories → explicit exit → sign-in/registration. Do not interrupt her account with registration or real external writes.

Mura lives in Göteborg; her Center story must include the ГБГ Форум context using authored illustrative people/messages, not copied real conversations. Online Center is allowed without inventing a physical venue. This explicitly supersedes an older blanket "no Center inside Mura" restriction; its underlying prohibition on fictional claims of a real venue remains.

For every implemented user-facing functional cluster, provide at least one linked Mura story and one real-user acceptance scenario. Do not force all stories through one enormous mandatory tour. Planned capabilities must remain distinguishable from shipped behavior. A simulated blockchain receipt must never be presented as an actual transaction.

## 10. Blockchain is a required workstream, not a universal dependency

The owner now requires blockchain in the integrated product. It must not silently return to an optional someday idea. Establish a concrete design, prototype and acceptance path for at least one genuinely blockchain-backed trust use case. No blockchain code is shipped by this charter.

Candidate uses to evaluate: versioned group agreements and decisions; multi-party confirmations; provenance of shared-resource/production records; cross-organisation commitments and portable roles; external integrity anchoring of appropriately designed records. These are engineering candidates, not settled implementation choices or proof that every use needs a ledger.

The fact that cooperation is desired does not establish that blockchain is technically necessary for every operation. Signatures, credentials, append-only records and blockchains are distinct tools. PostgreSQL remains operational truth. Real-world truth, valid authority and valid rights do not follow automatically from an immutable record.

Required design gates: named trust problem and participants; independent-verification benefit compared with simpler alternatives; public versus permissioned model and validator independence; privacy/linkability analysis; key management/recovery/revocation; authorisation; disputes/corrections; fees and funding; finality/reorganisation/outage behavior; threat model and specialist review; testnet proof and independent verification. Do not replace this workstream with a home-made hash-linked SQL table and label it blockchain.

Do not put profiles, private messages, identity numbers, home addresses, forum membership or sensitive requests onto public immutable ledgers. Hashing alone is not assumed to anonymise personal data. No mandatory wallet, seed phrase, token, NFT, financial custody or DAO governance is authorised. No public-chain deployment, fee spending or personal-data publication is authorised here. Ordinary civic/community functions must remain usable without chain availability. These are implementation safeguards, not cancellation of the blockchain requirement.

Maintain stable requirement IDs with: source/version and original wording; destination(s); present code evidence; implementation status; native/integration/human-operation route; dependencies; Mura scenario; real-user acceptance test; privacy/rights constraints; supersession decision if any.

Keep the prior 63-item audit intact as historical evidence. Its list is not proof of exhaustive coverage. Expand it with the fourth origin, current-code preservation, full Mura scope, SDCF, the retained Web3/Web4 capability family, blockchain and uncovered source details. Missing originals remain visible recovery gaps; reconstructions are labelled as reconstructions.

An origin is not fully integrated until its source inventory is accounted for and each active requirement has tested coverage. A mock, empty tab, draft schema, human promise, external link or passing unit test alone is not evidence of end-to-end delivery. Completeness of requirements and completeness of implementation must be reported separately.

## 12. Cross-origin acceptance journeys (design targets)

J1: a Göteborg question starts in the local Center/forum; a Host clarifies it; City provides an authoritative route or verified partner service; interested people coordinate a project; a participant reports the result; source/evidence limits remain visible.

J2: people aggregate a genuine need; combine skills and resources; plan work/production; choose supplier/purchase terms; organise joint output/sales and logistics; record agreements and qualified outcomes. Payments/accounting hand off explicitly to the proper system. A selected agreement can later demonstrate the approved ledger use without exposing personal data.

J3: someone arrives alone, joins ordinary conversation, discovers an activity, meets people, returns, and may later contribute. Belonging does not require a purchase, task or scored outcome.

J4: a future partner place/Inside/Node connects a resource and trained Host with an online request and follow-up. Until such a place exists, Mura may explain the future scenario without inventing current operation.

These are proposed test stories implementing the owner's scope, not claims about events already observed.

## 13. Implementation order without scope loss

Stage 1: preserve baseline and source originals; adopt this charter; reconcile conflicting pointers; complete requirement inventory.
Stage 2: design cross-domain object links, SDCF semantic mappings, stable object identities/derived Action Graph and permission contracts; map current implementation and gaps.
Stage 3: deliver online Center/forum context, Host/referral and partner-resource connections in vertical slices with Mura counterparts.
Stage 4: deliver cooperative-economic and governance slices, including sales/production/logistics/document functions and specialist handoffs; retain source-specific pending functions.
Stage 5: implement evidence-triggered Web3/Web4 slices (credentials/federation/agents/physical links as justified) and the reviewed blockchain trust prototype; no unsupported decentralisation or autonomy claims.
Stage 6: extend physical/partner formats and cross-city portability with real operators.

This sequence is an engineering proposal, not a new restriction on product scope. Dependencies may change order. Existing Auth, privacy, real-device and human-pilot gates still govern actual participant launch. No automatic Google activation, new subscription or expenditure is authorised.

## 14. Explicit supersessions

- Only three origins → four origins plus the existing implementation.
- Whole FOLKUNO equals only a physical Center → online and physical mechanisms distributed across FOLKOOP.
- Center necessarily means a building → online/physical/hybrid; ГБГ Форум is the Göteborg-local component.
- No Center anywhere inside Mura → online Center/forum stories required; no fabricated operating venue.
- Blockchain merely optional → required design/prototype/integration workstream with safety gates.
- A narrow pilot defines the whole product → pilot defines a validation slice only.
- Ban on former names even in provenance explanations → names permitted for origin traceability; FOLKOOP remains the sole product brand.

Unchanged: source truth, informed participation, participant privacy, rights attribution, existing licence boundaries, no counterfeit network activity, no automatic transfer of forum data, no claim that a status proves a real-world outcome.
