## Product thesis

FOLKOOP is a cooperation network built around:

`Intent -> Match -> Commit -> Coordinate -> Act -> Outcome -> Repeat`

The intended value is not another content feed. A person starts from something they need, can offer or want to do, and the system helps them reach relevant people/resources and a concrete next action.

Long-term product direction discussed and accepted includes:
- a compact daily/useful surface rather than attention-maximizing scrolling;
- truthful civic navigation and official-source handoffs;
- short useful action guides/tasks with symbolic progress before any monetary/reward economy;
- local cooperation, projects, resources and communities;
- physical/digital FOLKOOP places only when they actually exist;
- future trust/identity/agent layers only behind evidence and privacy gates.

No political preference profiling or political reward mechanics are part of the accepted direction.
