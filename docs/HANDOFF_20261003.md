## Product model

FOLKOOP is an action/cooperation operating system, not a feed-first social network.

Canonical loop:

`Intent → Match → Commit → Coordinate → Act → Outcome → Repeat`

Current primary navigation:
- Home
- Together
- Projects
- City
- Messages
- Me

City remains provenance/source-oriented. Center must not be presented as an operating physical venue unless a real venue/program exists.

Civic Teal remains the shared visual language.

## Mura immersion contract

The visitor-facing Mura experience is now a first-class product surface.

The intended flow is:

`Enter Mura → explore her people/projects/chats/communities/drafts/city/outcomes → explicitly leave Mura → only then show sign-in/registration`

Inside Mura:
- no signup/email pressure;
- no recurring pilot/demo/server/debug chrome;
- no Settings/About/Center detours;
- Mura remains read-only;
- people and activity in the story are illustrative local data, not claims about real live participants;
- the honest illustrative-story disclosure belongs before entry rather than repeatedly interrupting exploration.

The design goal is that users understand FOLKOOP by following one person's connected local life rather than reading a feature list.

### Current Mura story depth

The immersion pass expanded the local Mura story to roughly:

The important rule is connection density rather than raw count: new story objects should normally connect to at least two other objects such as a person, chat, community, project, resource or outcome.

## Mura screen principles

### Home
One immersive surface rather than generic Home subtabs.

Show:
- identity and Göteborg context;
- what is happening now;
- connected people;
- active needs/offers/projects/purchases;
- conversations;
- private ideas/drafts;
- completed outcomes;
- routes deeper into the account.

Do not end the screen with signup-funnel explanations.

### People
Show why Mura knows a person and the projects/chats/communities connecting them. Avoid treating People as a follower directory.

### Communities
Show what is happening there now, latest activity and why Mura returns.

### Messages
Show conversation context and story previews. Repetitive implementation/security/server copy should not dominate the normal Mura browsing experience; security/privacy facts belong in explicit information surfaces.

### Profile
Treat it as a personal life map: city, skills, current context, drafts and recent activity, not as an admin-style network-profile editor while in Mura.

### City
Mura is associated with Göteborg. Embedded Mura City should suppress demo/prototype/feedback chrome while preserving source/provenance truth.
